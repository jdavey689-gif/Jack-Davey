## Finding 1 (conceptual): Hallucination and error are different problems with different answers

The question "can hallucination be eliminated?" dissolved under debate into a sharper claim neither side started with:

> **Hallucination — unsupported content delivered in the confident, unmarked assertoric mode — is eliminable everywhere, by making provenance a mandatory type on every emitted claim. Error is eliminable only below the "checkable frontier" (claims with sound, non-neural checkers) and is irreducibly probabilistic above it — where it must be disclosed, measured per claim-class, and priced.**

Corollary that seeded everything after: **the lucrative artifact is not a non-hallucinating model; it is the certificate — an "epistemic warranty" with an actuarial table behind it.**

## Finding 2 (the company): The epistemic warranty is real only where truth is manufactured under an adversarial external oracle and sold to a buyer with a symmetric loss function

Three adversarial processes (Debate 2, blind scout 2b, and a live-web fact-check) converged on and then stress-tested one instantiation:

> **A neutral certification-and-warranty layer for AI-generated Medicare Advantage risk-adjustment (HCC) coding.** Per-code certificates — SUPPORTED (with calibrated residual risk) / UNSUPPORTED (delete before submission) / MISSED (supportable, uncaptured) — sold to at-risk entities (MA plans, full-risk provider groups) whose symmetric loss function (audit clawbacks and False Claims Act tail vs. lost capitation revenue) makes them the one buyer who pays for *accuracy* rather than *aggression*. Ground truth manufactured by blinded three-coder consensus panels on importance-sampled charts; payer-adjudication data used only to price drift and basis risk; warranty runs through an MGA with a fronting carrier (version-locked models, per-claim and aggregate caps, quarterly re-pricing).

**Why this survived when alternatives died:**
- The *legal-citation* wedge died: no feedback oracle (nobody grades filed briefs at scale) → no actuarial table → "warranty" is marketing on a commodity checker.
- The *general AI insurance* thesis died: correlated model-drift risk breaks diversification; human-in-the-loop lets enterprises self-insure; risk-bearing is capital-heavy.
- The *denial-prediction* framing died: the 835 remittance stream grades payer behavior, not coding truth — and Waystar already sells that.

**Fact-checked reality (mid-2026):**
- **White space confirmed:** no incumbent (Episource, Datavant/Apixio, Vatica, Reveleer, Navina, Advantasure) offers a calibrated certificate or insurance-backed warranty on coding output.
- **Neutrality moat documented:** Episource is owned by UnitedHealth/Optum — the most-scrutinized payer. You cannot be the defendant and the auditor.
- **Enforcement floor is live:** RADV extrapolation rule vacated Sept 2025 (CMS appealing), but CMS is auditing all ~550 eligible contracts with a 2,000-coder workforce; Kaiser settled a record $556M FCA case (Jan 2026); DOJ has a criminal investigation open into UnitedHealth's Medicare billing; OIG's Feb 2026 guidance explicitly flags AI-generated diagnoses and recommends auditing AI outputs.
- **Timing driver:** AI scribes (Ambience, Abridge, DAX) are shipping AI-suggested codes at scale *now*, and no one warrants their output.

**Honest sizing (co-signed by both debaters):** bear $8–15M / base $30–60M / bull $150–250M+ year-5 ARR. Venture-scale only in the bull case (certificate becomes the de facto standard demanded of every AI-coding vendor). An excellent focused company with a credible path to a big one — not a guaranteed mega-outcome.

**Six-month falsification tests (both required):** (1) *Epistemic:* certificate residual-risk numbers calibrate to blinded-panel consensus on held-out charts. (2) *Commercial:* at least two at-risk entities pay real money for output that includes revenue-reducing deletion recommendations. Either failure kills the thesis.

## Finding 3 (the investment thesis): The long-duration-storage parity line is pegged to gas capex, and gas capex just tripled

From the web-verified energy debate:

> **The canonical $20/kWh target for 100-hour storage is not an arbitrary cost goal — it is algebraically the new-gas-peaker parity line ($20/kWh × 100h = $2,000/kW). New peakers moved from ~$700–1,000/kW to ~$1,500–2,400/kW with 5–7 year turbine lead times, and PJM capacity has cleared at its price cap three consecutive auctions. The parity goalposts therefore moved 2–3× toward storage — and LDES valuations have not repriced for it.** Iron-air (Form Energy), at ~$77/kWh revealed pricing, needs roughly two learning doublings — fundable from its existing orderbook and the 50 GWh/yr Weirton ramp — to clear the *operative* threshold (~$40–50/kWh with 2028 delivery beats gas delivered 2031).

Deployment wedge: hybrid repowering (solar/wind + 4–8h lithium + 100h iron-air) at retiring thermal sites, reusing interconnection to bypass both queues; hyperscalers pay first. Six dated tripwires (in the debate file) make the thesis falsifiable; the surviving objections are inflated AI-load forecasts and single-company execution risk with zero complete utility-scale field winters.

## The meta-finding: what adversarial AI debate is actually good for

The night's honest epistemology, stated once: debate between AI instances did not prove a theorem or run an experiment. What it demonstrably did do — visible in the transcripts — is (1) kill four plausible-sounding theses that would each have wasted months (legal citations, general AI insurance, denial-prediction framing, naive 835 calibration); (2) force every surviving claim to carry named falsification conditions and dated tripwires; (3) converge independent blind processes onto the same structural answer, then correct that answer against live sources. The deliverable of adversarial synthesis is not discovery *ex nihilo* — it is a survivor: a thesis that has already absorbed the strongest attacks its authors could construct, with the tests attached that will tell you within six months whether it's real.
